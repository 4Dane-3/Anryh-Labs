# Cold call script: roofing companies, Washington

**Goal of every call:** a 15-minute call booked with the owner, plus their email, the contact's name and the CRM they use.
**Honesty rules:**
- Never invent jobs, clients, results, scarcity or what other agencies charge.
- Emma is an AI assistant: say so whenever it comes up.
- The only guarantee is the one below, exactly as written.

**The angle:** lead with *their calendar* (can they take more jobs?), not with us. Roofers don't buy "AI" or "agency". They buy booked inspections. The agency comparison is the second beat: it answers "why you and not the last guy who called me?"

## Opener
X: Hey, it's Dane, I'll be quick. You guys do roofing around [area], right?
C: [Responds]
X: Honest question: how's the schedule looking for the next month or two? Fully booked, or could you take on a few more replacement jobs?
C: [Responds]

- "We have room", "it's slowing down" or "maybe": go to **The pitch**.
- "We're fully booked": see **Objections**.
- "Who is this?" or "Is this marketing?": go to **The agency beat**, then **The pitch**.

## The pitch (about 30 seconds)
X: Got it. So we run Facebook and Google ads for roofers, under your company name. When a homeowner asks for a quote, Emma, our AI assistant, calls them within about a minute. She asks your questions (do they own the home, what's going on with the roof, roof type), and if they fit, she books the inspection straight onto your calendar. You just show up and quote.
X: Could your crew handle a few more of those a week?

## The agency beat
Use it when they say "I get calls from agencies all the time", "I already have a marketing guy" or "Is this marketing?". Say it plainly and don't knock anyone by name:
X: Yeah, I bet you get a few of those a week. Here's the difference. A lot of agencies charge a percentage of your ad spend, lock you into a contract and hand you a list of names to chase. We charge one flat fee, there's no contract, and you're not chasing anyone: Emma calls every homeowner within a minute and books the ones who fit. Because the AI does the busywork, we can charge a lot less than a full agency team.
X: And if three months go by without a paying job, the next three months are on us.

Don't quote what agencies charge unless the roofer tells you what they pay. Then compare against *their* number.

## Gatekeeper / office manager
X: No problem. Who handles marketing and where your jobs come from: the owner?
X: Could you put me through for two minutes, or when's a good time to catch them?
If they want a message: "It's Dane, about getting more replacement jobs booked onto your calendar. I'll try back [day]." Get the owner's name.

## "Where do the jobs come from?"
X: Two places, both under your name:
- **Google:** people in your area searching "roof replacement [city]" or "roof leak repair" right now. They're the hottest leads, because they're already looking.
- **Facebook and Instagram:** homeowners nearby who aren't searching yet, with videos of you or your crew at a jobsite, and your certifications, warranty and current special on screen. You don't film anything: one photo is enough.
X: Every ad goes to a page on your own website address, like book.yourroofing.com, with your logo and colours. Your main site isn't touched.

### "So it's Facebook leads?"
X: They come from our ads, yeah. The difference is you're not handed a name and a number to chase. Emma calls them within a minute, checks them against your rules, and only the ones who fit get booked onto your calendar.

### "What kind of jobs?"
X: Whatever you want. You tell us what you take: full replacements only, roof types, a minimum job size, homeowners only, insurance claims or not. Emma only books those.

### "Is Emma a real person?"
X: No, she's an AI voice assistant, and she tells people that if they ask. She calls in seconds instead of hours, never forgets a follow-up, and if someone wants a person, she passes them to you.

### "What if they submit at night?"
X: Emma only calls between 8am and 8pm their time. Anyone who asks for a quote after hours gets called first thing in the morning.

### "Do I have to film anything?"
X: No. Send one clear photo and we put you at a jobsite in the video. If you'd rather film yourself, we write the script and edit it. You approve every video before it runs.

## "How much?"
X: It depends on how many jobs you want a month, because that sets your ad budget. That's what the call's for: we look at your numbers and see if it makes sense. If it doesn't, I'll tell you straight.
X: Does [day] at 3 work?

### If they push for a ballpark
Give the real numbers. Don't dodge twice.
X: Fair enough. Two parts.
X: One, your ad budget. It's at least $2,000 a month for Facebook, plus Google if you want it, at least $1,000 a month. It goes straight to Facebook and Google on your own card: we don't take a cut.
X: Two, a one-time $1,000 setup fee, then a flat $1,500 a month for us. That's everything: the Facebook ads, the Google ads, the videos, Emma calling and booking your homeowners, and the reporting. No contract.
X: On a replacement averaging [$12–15k], one closed job covers months of it.

### "Is there a setup fee?"
X: Yes, $1,000 once. You pay it with your first month when we start, and your ads are billed by Facebook to your own card. You can cancel any month.

## The guarantee
Say it once they're interested, word for word:
X: And here's how we back it up. Give it three months. If the ads haven't brought you a single paying job by then, we work the next three months for free: no $1,500 fee, you only pay your ad budget.
X: All we ask is that the ads keep running at the minimum budget, you show up to the inspections Emma books, and you mark which ones you closed, so we both see the same numbers.

## Booking the call
X: Let's get you booked for a quick 15 minutes to see what it would look like and whether it makes sense.
X: Tomorrow's full for me. How's [day after tomorrow] at 3pm?
C: [Yes, or another time]
X: Perfect. What's the best email for the invite? You'll get an email with a Confirm button. Hit that so I know it's locked in.
X: And will I be speaking with you, or someone else on your team?
X: Last thing: what do you use to schedule jobs, JobNimbus, AccuLynx, something else? Then I can show you how bookings would get to you.
X: Awesome. Talk [day] at 3, [name].

Log on every booked call: the email, the contact name (in the "Contact" field) and their CRM (in the notes). The invite with its Confirm button goes to that email.

## Objections

**"We're fully booked."**
X: That's a good problem. Is that steady work, or a busy patch? A lot of guys are slammed in summer and quiet by November. This keeps the calendar full year-round, and when you're busy you just pause.

**"I've been burned by marketing before."**
X: Yeah, most guys have. What happened last time?
Let them talk. Whatever they complain about (junk leads, chasing people, no-shows, contracts, a cut of ad spend) is your pitch for the meeting.
X: That's exactly why it's a flat fee instead of a cut of your ad spend, there's no contract, Emma calls every lead within a minute so nobody goes cold, and if three months go by without a paying job, the next three months are free.

**"I get calls from agencies every day."**
Go to **The agency beat**.

**"Can you guarantee jobs?"**
X: Nobody can honestly promise a number of jobs. What we do promise: if the first three months don't bring you a paying job, the next three months of our work are free. You only pay your ads.

**"Do you have results or other roofers using it?"**
X: [Only real, current examples. If there aren't any yet:] We're newer in roofing, so I won't throw made-up numbers at you. That's why there's the guarantee and no contract: you'll see your own numbers in the first few weeks.

**"How fast does it work?"**
X: Facebook ads usually go live within a few days of setup, once Meta approves them. Google goes live once your Google Ads account is linked and your book. address is set up, which is one setting your web person adds. The first couple of weeks are testing to find which ads bring in the right homeowners, and it gets better from there.

**"That's a lot of ad spend."**
X: I get it. Below $2,000, Facebook doesn't get enough to learn from, and you'd be paying us to run ads too small to work. Google's optional: start with Facebook and add Google once you see it working.

**"I use Angi or HomeAdvisor."**
X: Makes sense. Are those jobs exclusive to you, and are you happy with what they cost? Ours come from ads under your name only, so the homeowner isn't getting calls from four other roofers.

**"Who owns the ad accounts?"**
X: You do. The ads run from your own Facebook and Google ad accounts and the budget is yours. If you ever leave, everything stays with you.

**"Just send me some info."**
X: I can, but it won't mean much without your numbers. It's 15 minutes, and if it doesn't make sense, you've lost 15 minutes. How's [day] at 3?

**"Not interested."**
X: No worries. Just out of curiosity, is that because you've got enough work, or because you've tried something like this before?
If they answer, you're back in the conversation. If not, thank them and move on.

**"Is this AI? I don't want a robot talking to my customers."**
X: Fair. Emma's upfront that she's an AI assistant, she sounds natural, and her only job is getting the inspection booked fast, before they call the next roofer. Anyone who wants a person gets passed to you. You'll hear her on the call before anything goes live.

## Voicemail
X: Hey [name], it's Dane. I help roofing companies in [area] get replacement jobs booked straight onto their calendar, from Google and Facebook ads under their own name. Worth a quick chat if you've got room for more work. I'll try you again [day], or call me back at [number].

## Additional services (once they're interested)
- **Your special stays current:** every week Migz checks your special is still running and replaces the ads if it changed.
- **Ads that beat your competitors:** before your Google campaign starts, Migz studies the roofers advertising in your area and writes ads that have to beat theirs in testing before they go live.
- **Reviews and referrals:** after each job we follow up to ask for a Google review and for referrals. Customers who refer 5 people who might need roofing get a $100 gift card. Never offer anything for a review: Google doesn't allow it.
