# Cold call script: roofing companies, Washington

## Opener
X: Hey, it's Dane, just giving you a quick ring. You guys are in Washington, right?
C: [Responds]
X: We work with one roofing company per area, and we've got room for one more in yours. Most guys we call are booked out. Are you fully booked right now, or could you take on a few more replacement jobs?
C: [Responds]

- "We have room" or "maybe": go to Booking the call.
- They ask a question: use the matching answer below, then come back to booking.
- "We're fully booked": see Objections.

## If they ask what kind of jobs
C: What kind of jobs are they?
X: Mostly high-ticket: full roof replacements and bigger repairs. You tell us exactly what you take (replacements only, roof types, minimum job size, homeowners only, insurance claims or not) and only those get booked.

## If they ask where the jobs come from
C: Where are you getting these from?
X: We run Meta ads in your area, under your name. The ads are videos of you, or whoever you want, standing at a job, with your certifications, your warranty and your current special on screen. You don't film anything. One photo is enough.
X: When a homeowner responds, our AI assistant Emma calls them within a minute, asks your qualifying questions and books the ones who fit straight into your CRM calendar. JobNimbus, AccuLynx, Roofr, whatever you use. You just show up and close.
X: I'm just wondering, could your crew handle a few more of those right now, or no?

### If they push: "So it's Facebook leads?"
X: The homeowner comes in from our ads, yeah. The difference is you never get a name and a number to chase. Emma calls them right away, while they still remember the ad, checks they fit your rules and puts the inspection in your calendar. If they don't fit, they never make it there.

### If they ask whether Emma is a real person
X: Emma's an AI voice assistant. She sounds natural, calls in seconds instead of hours, and never forgets a follow-up. If someone wants a person, she passes them to you.

## If they ask how much
C: How much does it cost?
X: It depends mostly on how many jobs you want a month, because that sets your ad budget. That's what the call's for: we look at your numbers and see if it even makes sense. If it doesn't, I'll tell you straight.
X: Does [day] at 3 work?

### If they push for a ballpark
Give one number. Don't dodge twice.
X: Fair enough. Two parts. Your ad budget goes straight to Meta, and every dollar of it goes to your ads: we don't take a percentage. Then it's a flat $1,000 a month for us to run everything: the ads, the videos, the testing, your reports. No setup fee, no contract.
X: Emma, the assistant who calls and books your homeowners, is $500 a month on top, if you want her.
X: So at, say, [$3,000] a month in ads, you're at [$4,000] with us, or [$4,500] with Emma. On a replacement averaging [$12–15k], one closed job a month covers all of it. Everything after that is profit.

### If they ask about a setup fee
X: There isn't one. You pay the first month when we start, and you can cancel any month.

## Booking the call
X: Well, let's get you booked in for a quick chat to see what it would look like, and whether it even makes sense for you.
X: Tomorrow's out, I'm booked up. Let's do [day after tomorrow] at 3pm. Does that work?
C: [Yes, or another time]
X: Perfect. What's the best email to send the invite to? You'll get an email with a confirm button. Just hit that so I know it's locked in.
X: And who will I be speaking with, you or someone else on your team?
X: Last one: what do you use to schedule jobs, JobNimbus, AccuLynx, something else? That way I can show you how the bookings land in it.
X: Awesome. Talk [day] at 3, [name].

Get the email, the contact's name and their CRM on every booked call. The contact name goes in the "Contact" field, the invite with its Confirm button goes to that email, and the CRM goes in the notes.

## Objections
**"We're fully booked."**
X: That's a good problem. Is that steady work coming in, or a busy patch? Most guys are slammed in summer and dead by November. This is how they keep the calendar full year-round. And when you're busy, you can pause.

**"I've been burned by marketing before."**
X: Yeah, most guys have. That's why we don't sell leads. Every homeowner gets a call within a minute and is checked against your rules before anything hits your calendar. And it's a flat monthly fee, no percentage, no contract. What happened last time?
Let them talk. Whatever they complain about (junk leads, chasing people, no-shows, long contracts) is your pitch for the meeting.

**"My videos/ads never work" or "I don't have time to film."**
X: You don't film anything. Send one photo and we put you at a jobsite in the video, with your certifications, warranty and special on screen. You approve every video before it runs.

**"Just send me some info."**
X: I can, but honestly it won't mean much without your numbers. It's a 15-minute call. If it doesn't make sense, you've lost 15 minutes. How's [day] at 3?

**"Not interested."**
X: No worries. Just out of curiosity, is that because you've got enough work, or because you've tried something like this before?
If they answer, you're back in the conversation. If not, thank them and move on.

**"Are you in marketing?"**
X: We are. But we don't hand you a list. We run the ads, our AI assistant qualifies every homeowner within a minute and books them into your calendar, for a flat monthly fee instead of a cut of your ad spend.

## Additional services
- **Your special stays current:** every week Migz checks your special is still running, and swaps out the ads if it changed.
- **Reviews and referrals:** follow-up after each job for a 5-star Google review and referrals ($100 gift card when a customer refers 5 people who might need roofing).
