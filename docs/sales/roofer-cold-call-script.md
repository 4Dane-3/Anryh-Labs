# Cold call script: roofing companies, Washington

**Goal of every call:** a 15-minute call booked with the owner, plus their email, the contact's name and the CRM they use.
**Honesty rules:** never invent jobs, clients, results or scarcity. Emma is an AI assistant: say so whenever it comes up. The only guarantee is the one below, exactly as written.

## Opener
X: Hey, it's Dane, I'll be quick. You guys do roofing in Washington, right?
C: [Responds]
X: I'm calling a few roofing companies around [area]. We run ads that bring in homeowners for replacements and repairs and book them straight onto your calendar. Most guys I talk to are either slammed or have a gap coming up. Where are you at right now: fully booked, or could you take on a few more jobs?
C: [Responds]

- "We have room" or "maybe": go to **Booking the call**.
- They ask a question: answer with the matching section, then come back to booking.
- "We're fully booked": see **Objections**.

## Gatekeeper / office manager
X: No problem. Who handles marketing and where your jobs come from: the owner?
X: Could you put me through for two minutes, or when's a good time to catch them?
If they want a message: "It's Dane, about getting more replacement jobs booked onto your calendar. I'll try back [day]." Get the owner's name.

## "What kind of jobs?"
X: Whatever you want. You tell us what you take: full replacements only, roof types, a minimum job size, homeowners only, insurance claims or not. Only those get booked. The ads offer a free roof inspection or a free quote, whichever you prefer.

## "Where do the jobs come from?"
X: Facebook and Instagram ads in your area, under your company name, run from your own Meta ad account. The ads are videos of you, or whoever you want, at a jobsite, with your certifications, warranty and current special on screen. You don't film anything: one photo is enough, and your real job photos make them even better.
X: When a homeowner responds, they pick a time for the inspection on your calendar. If you add Emma, our AI assistant, she calls them within about a minute, asks your qualifying questions and books the ones who fit. You just show up and close.
X: Could your crew handle a few more of those right now?

### "So it's Facebook leads?"
X: They come from our ads, yeah. The difference is you're not handed a name and a number to chase. They're booked onto your calendar, checked against your rules. If they don't fit, they never get there.

### "Is Emma a real person?"
X: No, she's an AI voice assistant, and she tells people that if they ask. She calls in seconds instead of hours, never misses a follow-up, and if someone wants a person, she passes them to you.

### "Do I have to film anything?"
X: No. Send one clear photo and we put you at a jobsite in the video. If you'd rather film yourself, we write the script and edit it. You approve every video before it runs.

## "How much?"
X: It depends on how many jobs you want a month, because that sets your ad budget. That's what the call's for: we look at your numbers and see if it makes sense. If it doesn't, I'll tell you straight.
X: Does [day] at 3 work?

### If they push for a ballpark
Give the real numbers. Don't dodge twice.
X: Fair enough. Two parts. Your ad budget, at least $2,000 a month, goes to Meta, and all of it goes to your ads: we don't take a percentage. Then it's a flat $1,000 a month for us to run everything: the ads, the videos, the testing, the reporting. No setup fee, no contract.
X: Emma, the assistant who calls and books your homeowners, is $500 a month on top, if you want her.
X: So at $2,000 in ads you're at $3,000 a month all in, or $3,500 with Emma. On a replacement averaging [$12–15k], one closed job covers about four months of everything.

### "Is there a setup fee?"
X: No. You pay the first month when we start, plus your first month of ads, $2,000 at the minimum. You can cancel any month.

## The guarantee
Say it once they're interested, word for word:
X: And here's how we back it up. Give it three months. If the ads haven't brought you a single paying job by then, we work the next three months for free: no $1,000 retainer, you only pay your ad budget.
X: All we ask is that the ads keep running at $2,000 a month or more, you show up to the inspections we book, and you mark which ones you closed, so we both see the same numbers.

If they ask about Emma: "The guarantee covers our retainer. Emma's a separate add-on, so she's billed as normal."

## Booking the call
X: Let's get you booked for a quick 15 minutes to see what it would look like and whether it makes sense.
X: Tomorrow's full for me. How's [day after tomorrow] at 3pm?
C: [Yes, or another time]
X: Perfect. What's the best email for the invite? You'll get an email with a Confirm button. Hit that so I know it's locked in.
X: And will I be speaking with you, or someone else on your team?
X: Last thing: what do you use to schedule jobs, JobNimbus, AccuLynx, something else? Then I can show you how bookings would get to you.
X: Awesome. Talk [day] at 3, [name].

Log on every booked call: the email, the contact name (in the "Contact" field) and their CRM (in the notes). The invite with its Confirm button goes to that email.

## Objections

**"We're fully booked."**
X: That's a good problem. Is that steady work, or a busy patch? A lot of guys are slammed in summer and quiet by November. This keeps the calendar full year-round, and when you're busy you just pause.

**"I've been burned by marketing before."**
X: Yeah, most guys have. What happened last time?
Let them talk. Whatever they complain about (junk leads, chasing people, no-shows, contracts, a cut of ad spend) is your pitch for the meeting.
X: That's why it's a flat fee instead of a cut of your ad spend, there's no contract, and if three months go by without a paying job, the next three months of our work are free.

**"Can you guarantee jobs?"**
X: Nobody can honestly promise a number of jobs. What we do promise: if the first three months don't bring you a paying job, the next three months of our work are free. You only pay your ads.

**"Do you have results or other roofers using it?"**
X: [Only real, current examples. If there aren't any yet:] We're newer in roofing, so I won't throw made-up numbers at you. That's why there's the guarantee and no contract: you'll see your own numbers in the first few weeks.

**"How fast does it work?"**
X: Ads usually go live within a few days of setup, once Meta approves them. The first couple of weeks are testing to find which ads bring in the right homeowners, and it gets better from there.

**"$2,000 a month is a lot."**
X: I get it. Below that, Meta doesn't get enough to learn from, and you'd be paying us to run ads too small to work. At $2,000 it's about $67 a day, and one replacement covers months of it.

**"I already have a marketing guy / I use Angi or HomeAdvisor."**
X: Makes sense. Are those jobs exclusive to you, and are you happy with what they cost? Ours come from ads under your name only, so the homeowner isn't getting calls from four other roofers.

**"Who owns the ad account?"**
X: You do. The ads run from your own Meta ad account and the budget is yours. If you ever leave, everything stays with you.

**"Just send me some info."**
X: I can, but it won't mean much without your numbers. It's 15 minutes, and if it doesn't make sense, you've lost 15 minutes. How's [day] at 3?

**"Not interested."**
X: No worries. Just out of curiosity, is that because you've got enough work, or because you've tried something like this before?
If they answer, you're back in the conversation. If not, thank them and move on.

**"Are you in marketing?"**
X: We are. But we don't sell you a list. We run the ads under your name and book homeowners into your calendar, for a flat monthly fee instead of a cut of your ad spend, and we back it with a guarantee.

## Voicemail
X: Hey [name], it's Dane. I help roofing companies in [area] get replacement jobs booked straight onto their calendar from ads under their own name. Worth a quick chat if you've got room for more work. I'll try you again [day], or call me back at [number].

## Additional services (once they're interested)
- **Your special stays current:** every week Migz checks your special is still running and replaces the ads if it changed.
- **Reviews and referrals:** after each job we follow up to ask for a Google review and for referrals. Customers who refer 5 people who might need roofing get a $100 gift card. Never offer anything for a review: Google doesn't allow it.
