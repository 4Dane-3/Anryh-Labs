# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: independent life insurance agents in the US who need more booked calls with real prospects. Today they buy leads, run their own Facebook and Instagram ads with mixed results, or do neither because ads eat time. They are not marketers. They judge advertising by one number: calls on the calendar, and what each one cost.

Secondary: prospects who land on an agent's booking page from an ad. Adults in the agent's licensed states, usually on a phone, thinking about coverage for family, a mortgage, or final expenses, and wary of being pressured.

## Product Purpose

anryh labs is an AI ad automation agency. Its platform, Migz AI, launches and manages Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ad campaigns for a life insurance agent: it makes or uses creatives (agent uploads, generated videos and images), tests new hooks on winners, adjusts budgets, placements and ads as results come in, and reports what it changed and why. Each agent also gets a booking page with a calendar and optional prep questions, and a daily Slack summary. Success is more booked calls per dollar.

## Positioning

Migz optimizes for booked calls (a Schedule conversion the booking page reports to Meta), not clicks or leads. It works inside Meta's Financial Products and Services rules (no age, gender or ZIP targeting), so creative and copy do the targeting. Every change is logged with its reason, and the agent can approve each change or let it run on autopilot within hard guardrails (budget cap, learning-phase protection, ad copy checked against insurance advertising rules).

## Operating Context

Meta Ads Manager and its learning phase; the agent's licensed states; Slack for morning summaries; a booking calendar; prospects answering optional prep questions (coverage, budget, goals) before a call.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Built: booking pages, prep form with editable questions, agent dashboard (overview, calendar, clients, ad funds, creatives, page editor, settings), the Migz review loop and guardrails, Higgsfield creative jobs, Conversions API events.
Not yet live: connections to real Meta ad accounts, Higgsfield and Stripe. No agents are running ads on the platform yet.
Insurance advertising rules apply: no guarantees of approval, rates or savings, no implying anyone qualifies.

## Brand Commitments

Name: anryh labs, always lowercase. The AI is Migz AI. Contact: anryhlabs@gmail.com. The product is invite only. The primary action is “reach out” (opens an email to request an invite). Migz runs the whole campaign, reading analytics and fixing budget, placements, creative and copy (not audience targeting); creatives are one lever among several. The user asked for a white look on the marketing site and for it to feel alive, with real motion and graphics, and not look like generated AI sites. The site speaks in lowercase.

## Evidence on Hand

None that is real: no customers, testimonials, logos, results or benchmarks exist yet and none may be invented. The product itself is real and can be demonstrated through sample activity, which must be labelled as an example. The sample "what migz changed" feed in the dashboard demo data is synthetic.

## Product Principles

1. Booked calls are the only number that matters; show the product pursuing it.
2. Every automated change comes with a reason the agent can read.
3. The agent stays in control: approval mode, hard limits, pause anytime.
4. Show the real mechanism working; never claim results that do not exist.
5. Speak like a trusted local professional to someone who is busy and skeptical of marketing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Honor prefers-reduced-motion everywhere; keep text contrast at WCAG AA; pages must work on a phone with a thumb, since both agents and prospects arrive from phones.
